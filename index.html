Meet the Author

Dr. Ashish Gummadi is a physiotherapist who earned his Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.P.T) at Christian Medical College (C.M.C) Vellore, India, and his Master of Physiotherapy (M.P.T) at RGUHS (Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences), Bangalore, India — credentials assessed in Canada as equivalent to a bachelor's degree plus a master's degree. He now lives in Nova Scotia, Canada.

His clinical books grow straight out of that training and practice: The Physio Playbook, The Clinical Tests Companion (Orthopedic) — 105 special tests across 9 regions — The Neuro Companion (Neurologic), and The Community Companion (Community Physiotherapy), each one written from scratch to give physiotherapy students a companion he wishes he had had.

He also writes practical books for people who are tired of feeling stuck. He brings the same steady, step-by-step thinking from rehabilitation into everyday life: small, honest steps — the kind that survive busy weeks and bad days. He believes change does not require a new personality, only better-designed days. His readers describe his style as warm, direct, and refreshingly free of hype. That side of his writing is The Mind Reset and The Ultimate You: The Art of Being Human When Life Breaks You.

Ashish founded Gabriella Press, his independent publishing house in Nova Scotia, and named it for his daughter Gabriella — the press is his to build, and one day, hers to inherit. Follow his books on his Amazon author page.

About Gabriella Press

Gabriella Press is an independent Canadian publisher founded by Dr. Ashish Gummadi in Nova Scotia, and named for his daughter Gabriella — he plans to hand the press to her when she turns 18. We believe books should be original, beautiful, and made with care — no copied content, ever.

Eight titles are published so far: Gabriella's two children's books, two guides for the mind and everyday life, and four clinical companions for physiotherapy students and clinicians. Every title is crafted from scratch, and readers can find them on Amazon, through Pothi in India, on Kobo, and in libraries through OverDrive.
